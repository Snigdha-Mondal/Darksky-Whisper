## 2. Prominent Asterisms & Seasonal Reference Frames

### The Summer Triangle (Northern Summer / Autumn)
* **Components**: **Vega** (Lyra, mag 0.0), **Altair** (Aquila, mag 0.8), **Deneb** (Cygnus, mag 1.25).
* **Observation**: Spans across the Milky Way dust lanes overhead from July to November.
* **Orientation Cue**: Deneb forms the tail of Cygnus the Swan flying south along the galactic plane.

### The Winter Hexagon (Northern Winter / Spring)
* **Components**: **Sirius** (Canis Major), **Rigel** (Orion), **Aldebaran** (Taurus), **Capella** (Auriga), **Pollux** (Gemini), **Procyon** (Canis Minor).
* **Centerpiece**: **Betelgeuse** sits inside the center of the hexagon, creating the compact **Winter Triangle** with Sirius and Procyon.

### The Great Square of Pegasus (Northern Autumn)
* **Components**: **Alpheratz** ($\alpha$ And), **Scheat** ($\beta$ Peg), **Markab** ($\alpha$ Peg), **Algenib** ($\gamma$ Peg).
* **Observation Guide**: High in the southern sky during October and November. Used to locate the Andromeda Galaxy (M31) two hops northeast of Alpheratz.

### The Big Dipper (Ursa Major Asterism)
* **Components**: Alkaid, Mizar/Alcor, Alioth, Megrez, Phecda, Merak, Dubhe.
* **Pointer Stars**: Merak and Dubhe form the outer wall of the bowl. Extending an imaginary line 5 times the distance between them points directly to **Polaris** ($\alpha$ UMi).

## 3. Star-Hopping Guide for Spoken Guidance

When generating conversational cues for observers, use relative directional hops rather than coordinate degrees alone:

1. **Finding Polaris from Ursa Major**:
   *"Follow the two outer bowl stars of the Big Dipper straight across five times their distance to locate the steady glow of Polaris."*
2. **Finding Sirius & Aldebaran from Orion's Belt**:
   *"Follow the three stars of Orion's belt downward toward the horizon to meet Sirius, the brightest star in the night sky. Trace the belt upwards in the opposite direction to reach orange Aldebaran and the Pleiades."*
3. **Arcing to Arcturus & Speeding to Spica**:
   *"Follow the curved handle of the Big Dipper: arc to bright amber Arcturus, then speed on to sapphire Spica near the horizon."*
